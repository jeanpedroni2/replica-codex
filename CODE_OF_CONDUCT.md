# Community Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, harassment and discriminatory conduct are not.

Report concerns privately to repository maintainers using the contact channels they choose to publish. Maintainers may moderate, hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and mindful of privacy.
